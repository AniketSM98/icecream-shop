# Daily Operations Guide

Simple guide for shop staff. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## Opening the Shop

1. Turn on the laptop
2. Double-click **"Start App"** on the Desktop
3. Three windows will open — two black command windows and Chrome
4. Wait about 10 seconds for everything to load
5. The app opens automatically in Chrome at the Dashboard
6. You are ready to start recording sales

**If the app does not open after 15 seconds:**
- Close everything and double-click "Start App" again
- If still not working, call the owner

---

## Recording a Sale

1. Click **Sales** in the top menu
2. For each item the customer is buying:
   - Select the **Category** (e.g. Cone, Cup)
   - Select the **Product** (e.g. Chocolate Cone)
   - Enter the **Quantity**
   - Price fills in automatically
3. To add more items to the same sale, click **+ Add Item**
4. If giving a discount on the total, enter it in **Discount on Total**
5. Select payment: **CASH** or **UPI**
6. Check the **Total** amount
7. Click **Record Sale**
8. You will see a green success message with the total amount

---

## Correcting a Wrong Sale

If you made a mistake recording a sale:

1. Go to the **Sales** page
2. Find the wrong sale in the **Recent Sales** table at the bottom
3. Click the **Delete** button on that row
4. Confirm when asked
5. The sale is removed and stock is restored automatically
6. Record the correct sale again

---

## Restocking (Adding Stock)

When new stock arrives:

1. Click **Inventory** in the top menu
2. Find the product in the list
3. Click **Update Stock** button on that row
4. Enter the **new total quantity** (not the amount added — the full new amount)
5. Click **Save**
6. The quantity updates immediately

---

## Checking Low Stock

1. The **Dashboard** shows a red "Low Stock Alert" card if any items are running low
2. Click **Inventory** to see the full list — low stock items are highlighted in red
3. Restock those items before they run out

---

## Closing the Shop

1. Make sure all sales for the day are recorded
2. Double-click **"Stop App"** on the Desktop
3. The command windows and Chrome will close automatically
4. You can now close the laptop

---

## Checking Today's Sales (Dashboard)

1. Click **Dashboard** in the top menu
2. You will see:
   - Total money collected today
   - Number of transactions
   - Cash vs UPI split
   - Best selling products
   - Low stock items
3. Click **Refresh** to update the numbers

---

## Important Notes

- Always use **Stop App** to close — do not just close the Chrome window
- Do not close the two black command windows while working — the app needs them
- If the laptop restarts or shuts down unexpectedly, just double-click **Start App** again
- All sales data is saved automatically — nothing is lost if the app closes unexpectedly
