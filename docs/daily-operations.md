# Daily Operations Guide

Simple guide for shop staff. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## Opening the Shop

1. Turn on the laptop
2. Double-click **"Start App"** on the Desktop
3. Three windows will open — two black command windows and Chrome
4. Wait about 10 seconds for everything to load
5. The app opens automatically in Chrome at the Dashboard
6. You are ready to start recording sales

**If the app does not open after 15 seconds:**
- Close everything and double-click "Start App" again
- If still not working, call the owner

---

## Recording a Regular Sale (Cash or UPI)

1. Click **Sales** in the top menu
2. For each item:
   - Select the **Category**
   - Select the **Product**
   - Enter the **Quantity**
   - Price fills automatically
3. Click **+ Add Item** to add more products to the same sale
4. Enter discount if giving one (optional)
5. Select **CASH** or **UPI**
6. Check the **Total** amount
7. Click **Record Sale**

---

## Recording a Credit Sale (Udhaar)

1. Go to **Sales** page
2. Add items as usual
3. Select **CREDIT (Udhaar)** as payment mode
4. Enter the **Customer Name**
5. Click **Record Sale**
6. The sale is recorded and the customer's balance increases

---

## Recording a Credit Payment

When a credit customer comes to pay:

1. Click **Credit** in the top menu
2. Find the customer in the list
3. Click **Record Payment**
4. Enter the amount they are paying (can be partial)
5. Select Cash or UPI
6. Click **Record Payment**
7. Their balance reduces automatically

---

## Using Voice Command (Sales Page)

1. Go to **Sales** page
2. Click the purple **Start Listening** button
3. Say your items clearly: *"2 chocolate cone, 1 mango cup, cash"*
4. Click **Listening... (Click to Stop)**
5. The app shows what it heard in a purple box
6. Click **Fill Form** — items fill in automatically
7. Check the form is correct
8. Click **Record Sale**

**Tips for voice command:**
- Speak clearly and not too fast
- Say quantity first, then product name: *"3 chocolate cone"*
- Separate items with a comma: *"2 chocolate, 1 mango"*
- Say "cash" or "upi" at the end
- If something is wrong, fix it manually in the form before recording

---

## Correcting a Wrong Sale

1. Go to **Sales** page
2. Find the wrong sale in the **Recent Sales** table
3. Click **Delete**
4. Confirm when asked
5. Record the correct sale again

---

## Restocking (Adding Stock)

1. Click **Inventory** in the top menu
2. Find the product
3. Click **Update Stock**
4. Enter the **new total quantity** (not the amount added — the full new amount)
5. Click **Save**

---

## Adding a Pre-order / Customer Demand

When a customer wants something you don't have:

1. Click **Pre-orders** in the top menu
2. Click **Add Pre-order**
3. Enter the product name (required) and any other details
4. Click **Add Pre-order**
5. When the item is available, update the status to **Ready**
6. Call the customer and update to **Delivered** when they collect

---

## Checking Today's Sales (Dashboard)

1. Click **Dashboard** in the top menu
2. You will see today's total sales, profit, cash/UPI split, and top products
3. Click **Refresh** to update the numbers

---

## Closing the Shop

1. Make sure all sales are recorded
2. Double-click **"Stop App"** on the Desktop
3. The command windows and Chrome will close
4. Close the laptop

**Important:** Always use Stop App — do not just close Chrome manually.
